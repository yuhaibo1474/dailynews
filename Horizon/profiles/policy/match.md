# Policy & regulation profile

Use this profile for government policy, regulation, legislation, and regulatory actions — including technology and AI policy, data governance, cybersecurity, antitrust, industrial policy, trade and export controls, and cross-border regulation.

Typical items include new laws or administrative rules, regulatory filings or enforcement actions, government plans or programs, official policy documents, and analysis of policy impact.

Do not use this profile for general company news, product launches, market-only coverage, or content whose primary value is technical explanation.
