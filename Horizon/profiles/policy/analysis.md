# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of policy, regulation, and government decisions for readers tracking technology, industry, and the economy.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Landmark.** Major legislation, landmark regulatory decisions, or policy shifts with broad economic or social impact.
- **7-8: High value.** Significant rules, regulatory reforms, enforcement actions, or policy announcements with material impact on industry or the public.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Noteworthy policy developments, consultations, or proposals with narrower scope.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine administrative matters, narrow approvals, or updates of limited relevance.
- **0-2: Noise.** Trivia (for example holiday schedules), promotional content, or items irrelevant to policy.

# Evaluation guidance

Judge by the scope of affected parties, regulatory significance, and likely impact on industries or the public. Prefer official sources and concrete details. Do not reward a loud headline; a routine holiday notice is noise even when it comes from an official source.
